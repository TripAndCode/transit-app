# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Transit Delay App ("the Service") is run by TripAndCode, an individual. This page explains what information about you the Service handles and how. The Japanese version of this policy is the authoritative one.

## Operator

- Name: TripAndCode (run by an individual)
- Full name and address: provided without delay on request.
- Contact: [contact form]({{CONTACT_FORM_URL}}). Replies come from an address used only for the Service. Bugs and requests are also welcome on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/TripAndCode/transit-app/issues), but Issues are public, so please don't include personal information there.

## What we collect and why

| Information | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Email address, display name, avatar image URL | When you sign in with Google or GitHub | To identify you and show your account |
| Sign-in history (time, method, IP address, browser type) | When you sign in and out | To investigate misuse and keep the Service secure |
| Sessions (IP address, browser type, last used) | While you use the Service | To keep you signed in, and to let you sign out other devices |
| Daily usage counts (requests and errors per screen or API route, and the operator you were viewing) | While you use the Service | To understand usage, manage load, and plan pricing |
| Saved filters and Ask conversations | When you save them | To provide those features |
| Ask questions, not linked to your account | When you use Ask | To improve answers |
| An LLM API key you register | When you register it | To use your key for Ask. It is stored encrypted |
| Names and dates of API keys issued to you | When the operator issues them | To provide the API |

The Service shows no ads and uses no analytics tools. It sets only two cookies: one that keeps you signed in, and a temporary one used only during sign-in. Display settings and saved analyses stay in your browser and are not stored on our servers.

## How long we keep it

| Information | Period |
|---|---|
| Your account, saved filters, Ask conversations, a registered LLM API key | Until you delete them |
| API keys issued to you, including revoked ones | Until you delete your account |
| Sessions | Up to 30 days |
| Sign-in history | 25 months |
| Daily usage counts | 25 months |
| Ask questions not linked to your account | 90 days |
| The admin action log (including actions on your account) | 400 days |

Records past their period are deleted automatically every day.

## Sending information to other companies

Except where the law requires it, we don't give your personal information to third parties without your consent. To run the Service, we have the following companies handle it on our behalf. All of them are based in the United States.

- **Railway Corporation:** servers and database.
- **Google LLC and GitHub, Inc.:** sign-in (only the one you choose).
- **Google LLC (Gemini API) and OpenAI, L.L.C.:** when an Ask question reaches the stage answered by an LLM, we send the question and the aggregate data needed to answer it. If you registered your own API key, it goes to that key's provider.

You can read about the United States' personal-information protection system in the Personal Information Protection Commission's survey of foreign systems (「外国における個人情報の保護に関する制度等の調査」).

## What you can do

- **Download your data:** from your account page, download everything the Service holds about you as JSON.
- **Delete your account:** from your account page, delete your account. Everything in the tables above that concerns you is removed immediately. Your email address and name in the admin action log are masked. Only Ask questions not linked to your account remain, until their 90 days pass.
- **Other requests:** requests for disclosure, correction, suspension of use and the like go to the contact above.

## How we keep it safe

- Connections are encrypted with HTTPS.
- Sign-in is handled by Google and GitHub; we never hold a password.
- A registered LLM API key is stored encrypted.
- Only the operator can see personal information.
- The Service runs on servers provided by a company based in the United States (Railway Corporation).

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we'll announce it on this page.
